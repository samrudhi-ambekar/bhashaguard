Eligibility & admission process

Candidates who have completed Class 10 or an equivalent examination may apply for certificate courses. Some advanced programmes require Class 12 or a relevant ITI qualification.

इयत्ता दहावी किंवा समकक्ष परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण केलेले विद्यार्थी प्रमाणपत्र अभ्यासक्रमांसाठी अर्ज करू शकतात. काही प्रगत अभ्यासक्रमांसाठी इयत्ता बारावी किंवा संबंधित आयटीआय पात्रता आवश्यक आहे.

Documents required

Class 10 marksheet, school leaving certificate, domicile proof and a recent photograph.

Application fee

No application fee for eligible candidates from reserved categories.

Selection basis

Merit list prepared from the qualifying examination marks.

How to apply

Follow these steps to submit an application through the demo portal.

  1. Create a candidate profile using your mobile number and email address.
  2. Select your preferred trade and institute from the available list.
  3. Upload the required documents in PDF or JPG format.
  4. Review your details and submit the application.
